# Roster rules — 2026-11-19

David: when a bot is deleted and replaced (c0), the new bot takes the old one's seat in Agent City and the retired `old N` bot is not shown. Dash/sub-number bots (`N-M. ...`, for example `5-1. Watermark Lab`) are also not shown.

These are now generic viewer rules (not a list of ids) in `BOTS.retiredName`, `BOTS.subBotName` and `BOTS.seatPrefix` in `public/office2d/config.js`, applied by `roster()` in `public/office2d/main.js`. `hiddenIds` and `spriteByNum` still work. Permanent copy: AGENTS.md, "Roster rules".

Examples at the time of the change: new `1. Grok` (11a07880) sits in seat 1, `old 1` (1903052e) is hidden; new `14. Dashboards` (e0bf5277) sits in seat 14, `old 14` (fa5eb849) is hidden; `4-1. Studio Box` and `5-1. Watermark Lab` are hidden.
